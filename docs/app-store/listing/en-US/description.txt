Hold down the right mouse button and a bubble pops up next to the pointer. Flick toward what you want, let go, and it's done.

A short click still opens the normal context menu. Only a long press brings up Pop, so right-dragging in games and 3D apps keeps working.

IT KNOWS WHAT YOU SELECTED
• Text in another language is translated right away, offline, with Apple's translation.
• Math is calculated, and units and colors are converted.
• Text in images is read with OCR, and you can take a screenshot and read the text in it.

ONE FLICK AND IT'S DONE
• Arrange more than a hundred actions on the ring the way you like and flick toward a slot to run it.
• Press 1–9 or 0 to pick a slot, Esc to close.
• Give your favorite actions their own keyboard shortcuts, or use a different ring in each app.

HANDY TOOLS
• Clipboard history you can search.
• Pin text or images to the screen, annotate screenshots, pick colors and measure the screen.
• Case conversion, JSON and code formatting, Base64 and URL encoding, timestamps, QR codes and much more.
• Image tools: convert, resize, crop and stitch images you select in Finder.
• AI polish, summaries and explanations with Apple's on-device model on macOS 26, or with your own OpenAI-compatible service and key.

ADD YOUR OWN ACTIONS
• Turn any website search into an action with a URL template.
• Process the selected text with a few lines of JavaScript.

INSTALL ONLY WHAT YOU USE
Many actions are separate plugins built into the app. Turn one on when you need it and off when you don't.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
Pop has no account, no servers, no analytics and no ads. Clipboard history stays on your Mac and API keys stay in your keychain.

PERMISSIONS
• Accessibility: to notice the long press of the right button and read the text you selected. Add Pop with + in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility; Pop shows you how.
• Folder access: to work on files you select in Finder, allow Pop once to access a folder, usually your home folder.
• Screen Recording: only for screenshots and the color picker, asked the first time you use them.

Pop is free and open source.
